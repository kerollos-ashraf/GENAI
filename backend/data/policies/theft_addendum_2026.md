# OmniCare Theft & Burglary Addendum 2026

## Section 3: Theft and Burglary Coverage

Theft of personal property following forced entry into the insured home is covered up to $5,000 per incident, with a $250 deductible. A police report must be filed within 48 hours of discovering the theft. Theft without signs of forced entry is excluded.

## Section 4: Temporary Accommodation

If the home is uninhabitable after a covered loss, hotel and temporary accommodation costs are reimbursed up to $150 per night for a maximum of 30 nights.
